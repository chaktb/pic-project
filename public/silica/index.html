Refractive Index of Fused Silica

Sellmeier equation (Malitson 1965), λ = 0.25 – 2.5 μm

Refractive index n
—

Sellmeier Equation

n²(λ) = 1 + Σᵢ Bᵢ·λ² / (λ² − Cᵢ)
B₁ = 0.6961663, C₁ = 0.0684043²  ·  B₂ = 0.4079426, C₂ = 0.1162414²  ·  B₃ = 0.8974794, C₃ = 9.896161²
