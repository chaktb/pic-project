Equations

α(R) = (γ/2)·exp(−2γ·a)·exp(−B·R),  B = 2γ³ / (3β²),  a = W/2
Loss(dB) = 4.343 · α(R) · R · θ    (arc length L = R·θ)
Loss has a peak at R = 1/B; the design-relevant root is on the right (large-R) side.
