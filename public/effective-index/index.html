Formulas

Sellmeier (Malitson 1965):
n²(λ) = 1 + Σᵢ Bᵢ·λ² / (λ² − Cᵢ)
Slab TE eigenvalue (fundamental):
u·tan(u) = w,   u² + w² = V²,   V = (k₀·d/2)·√(n_core² − n_clad²)
n_eff = √(n_core² − (2u/d)² / k₀²)
EIM (TE-TE): 1) vertical slab thickness H → n_eff_v    2) horizontal slab thickness W with core = n_eff_v → n_eff
