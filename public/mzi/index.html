Equations

Each DC: T = [[cos(κL), −j sin(κL)], [−j sin(κL), cos(κL)]]
Phase shift in arms: Δφ = β·ΔL = (2π·n_eff/λ)·ΔL
P_bar = |cos(κL₁)cos(κL₂) − sin(κL₁)sin(κL₂)·exp(−jΔφ)|²,  P_cross = 1 − P_bar
FSR ≈ λ² / (n_eff · ΔL) — channel spacing in WDM
